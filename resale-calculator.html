How the fees work

eBay charges its final value fee on the whole order, including the shipping and sales tax the buyer pays, plus a small per-order fee. Promoted listings add another percentage on top if you use them.

Mercari takes a flat percentage of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, with no separate processing fee.

Whatnot takes a commission on the item price only, then a payment processing fee on the full order. It's often the cheapest for mid-priced toys, but you need an audience for live shows.

Tips for better margins

Bundle low-value figures into lots so the flat per-order fees hurt less. Weigh and measure before listing so your shipping charge covers the real label. For anything over about $100, compare platforms. A couple of points of fees adds up fast.
