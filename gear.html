Why storage matters for value

Condition is the single biggest driver of resale value for comics, cards and boxed collectibles. A crease, a sun-faded spine or a crushed box corner can cut a price in half, and grading companies notice everything. A few dollars on the right sleeve, case or frame protects pieces that can be worth hundreds.
