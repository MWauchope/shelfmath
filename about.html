About ShelfMath

ShelfMath is run by Michael Wauchope.

ShelfMath builds simple, free calculators for people who collect and resell pop culture collectibles: comics, trading cards, action figures, vinyl figures, die-cast, building sets, costumes, props and more. Our goal is to make the math behind buying, grading, and selling quick and honest.

All calculations run in your browser. The tools give estimates, so always confirm current fees on each marketplace before you list.

ShelfMath is independent and not affiliated with any marketplace, grading company, or toy brand. We may earn a commission from some links, which helps keep the tools free.
