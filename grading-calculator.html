When grading usually pays off

Grading makes the most sense when there's a big price gap between top grades and everything else, and you're confident the piece is near-mint: sharp corners and centering on cards, flat spines and white pages on comics, and crisp, uncrushed packaging on figures. If the high-grade premium is small, or the item is worth less than a few times the grading fee, selling raw is usually smarter.

Don't forget

Grading takes weeks to months, and the market can move while you wait. Factor in selling fees too, since they scale with the higher graded price. The resale fee calculator shows you the payout.
